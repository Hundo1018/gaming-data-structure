# brute_force

Not a hypothesis. This is the oracle. It is measured on the same terms as every
candidate so that the cost of looking at everything is on the record, and so
that "faster than a loop over all entities" is never mistaken for a result.

Verifying it compares it against itself and proves nothing.

The one question it does answer: at what population does an index stop paying
for itself. `s05_small_world` exists to find that point.
